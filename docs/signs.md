# UK speed-limit graphics

The driving UI draws the maximum speed-limit roundel (TSRGD diagram 670) for a known static numeric limit, and the national speed-limit disc (diagram 671) when OSM explicitly marks a UK national limit. When the limit is missing, variable, conditional, lane-specific or ambiguous, it draws a neutral `--` placeholder instead of a legal sign.

Design reference: The Highway Code, Traffic signs, https://www.gov.uk/guidance/the-highway-code/traffic-signs and Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 2016, Schedule 10 Part 2, https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2016/362/schedule/10/part/2 . Sign graphics are reproduced in app code based on those references. © Crown copyright; reused under Open Government Licence v3.0, https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/ .

These are information graphics in the app. A displayed value is not an observation of a physical roadside sign, and posted road signs take precedence.
